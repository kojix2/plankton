audio.fft(0…31): 32 logarithmic bands, low to high. audio.level: RMS volume. Both return 0…1 and are zero while the microphone is off. Sound is not sent to speakers.

    

    

    
Camera and microphone start only after you enable them. Stop releases devices. Reloading or opening a shared link never enables them automatically. HTTPS or localhost is required.
